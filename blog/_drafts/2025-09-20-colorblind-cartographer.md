My wife worked as a babysitter on the side when we first started dating, and at one point she mentioned that her boyfriend (👋) was colorblind, and they immediately asked "Does he use a cane?"

The answer of course is that I don't, but that story illustrates the not-quite-a-disability confusion of the fact that I am a [proud?] Protanope. Like 2% of the male population, [I am fully missing the red-perceiving cone](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Color_blindness#Red%E2%80%93green_color_blindness) in what is supposed to be a three-channel sensor system in my eyes. As a result, I mostly can't see any difference in hue between a rose and the leaves around it, and Cookie Monster and Grimace appear to be the exact same color to me. This is not the world's biggest headache for the most part, except for when I'm in the woods, determining the safety of berries based on how closely they resemble gummy bears.

![2](/shoals/assets/img/2025-09-20_2.jpg)

However, some among you will have already detected a major issue, and perhaps asked yourselves: __"How does this deficiency affect his occupation?"__

I am here to tell you that I am a colorblind cartographer, and I am not alone.
