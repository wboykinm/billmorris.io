## Instant pot Haitian rice in lands with no Epis

- 2.5cups long grain rice (rinsed)
- 2 cans cooked red beans
- 1qt vegetable or chicken stock
- 1 can coconut milk
- 6ish cloves garlic
- 1tsp salt
- 0.5tsp black pepper
- 1tsp cayenne
- 0.5tsp onion powder
- Olive oil

1. Turn pot on manual and add oil and crushed garlic, cook for 1min
2. Add strained beans and all spices, sautee for another minute
3. Add rinsed rice and mix, sautee for one more minute
4. turn pot off, add stock and coconut milk, stir well to mix
5. Seal pot and run rice cycle, vent when finished
6. Fluff and enjoy :)
